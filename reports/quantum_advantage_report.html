Scientific Conclusion

Verdict: Demonstrated Quantum-Assisted Benefit (No Quantum Supremacy).

While classical Simulated Annealing outperforms simulated QAOA on runtimes and tight deadlines (due to circuit compilation and execution overhead), raw QAOA provides high search diversity (Shannon Entropy > 2.0). The Hybrid QAOA pipeline successfully leverages this diversity to search multiple separate candidate basins, matching global optimum quality as problem dimensions scale.
