MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & CORE FORMULAE

This center documents the complete mathematical foundations governing the physical simulation, AI predictions, multi-objective optimization (QUBO), Ising mapping, and closed-loop control quality gates.

1. Train Movement & Delay (Digital Twin)

Description: Simulates train movement every simulation tick by updating train speed based on congestion and calculating accumulated delay.

F_congestion = max(0.5, 1.0 − 0.15 × (N_trains − 1))
V_current = V_current × F_congestion
D_t = D_(t−1) + (V_base − V_current) / V_base
Symbol Definitions:
Fcongestion – Congestion speed reduction factor.
Ntrains – Number of trains on a track segment.
Vcurrent – Current train speed.
Vbase – Scheduled (baseline) train speed.
Vleading – Speed of the train ahead.
Dt – Delay at the current time step.
Dt−1 – Delay at the previous time step.
ΔP – Progress increment along the track.
dtrack – Length of the current track segment.
v – Train speed (km/h).
thour, tmin – Current hour and minute.
Irain – Rain intensity (0 = no rain, 1 = heavy rain).
Scomplexity – Route complexity score.
2. AI Delay Prediction

Description: Predicts future train delays and estimates the confidence interval of the prediction.

95% Prediction Interval = [ μ − 1.96σ , μ + 1.96σ ]
Symbol Definitions:
μ – Mean predicted delay.
σ – Standard deviation of ensemble predictions.
C – AI prediction confidence score.
Ppropagation – Delay propagation pressure at a station.
3. Multi-Objective Optimization (QUBO)

Description: Converts multiple railway objectives into a single optimization cost function.

Minimize
O(x) = Σ c_i x_i
c_i = Σ (w_k × normalized_metric_(k,i) × direction_k)
Symbol Definitions:
O(x) – Overall optimization objective.
xi – Binary decision variable (0 = not selected, 1 = selected).
ci – Cost of decision i.
wk – Weight assigned to objective k.
Q – QUBO cost matrix.
Pstrength – Constraint penalty strength.
pswitch – Stability penalty for changing decisions.
4. QUBO to Ising Mapping

Description: Transforms the classical QUBO optimization problem into an Ising Hamiltonian suitable for quantum computation.

x_i = (I − Z_i) / 2
H_Ising = Σ h_i Z_i + Σ J_ij Z_i Z_j + C_offset
Symbol Definitions:
HIsing – Ising Hamiltonian equivalent of the QUBO.
xi – Binary decision variable (0 = not selected, 1 = selected).
Zi – Pauli-Z operator acting on qubit i.
hi – Single-qubit (Z) coefficient.
Jij – Two-qubit interaction (ZZ) coefficient.
Coffset – Constant energy offset.
5. QAOA Quantum Optimization

Description: Applies alternating Cost and Mixer operators to search for the optimal railway dispatch solution.

|ψ(γ,β)⟩ = [ ∏ U_M(β_k) U_C(γ_k) ] |ψ₀⟩
Symbol Definitions:
|ψ0⟩ – Warm-start initial quantum state.
|ψ(γ,β)⟩ – QAOA quantum state after optimization layers.
γ – Cost Hamiltonian parameter.
β – Mixer Hamiltonian parameter.
p – Number of QAOA layers.
UC – Cost unitary.
UM – Mixer unitary.
E(γ,β) – Expected energy (objective value) of the quantum state.
M – Number of measurement shots.
6. Decision Quality Gate

Description: Accepts a newly optimized railway schedule only if it provides sufficient improvement over the current schedule.

ΔU = U_new − U_current − C_switch
or equivalently,
ΔU = E_current − E_new − C_switch

Accept New Plan if:
ΔU > 0.05
Symbol Definitions:
ΔU – Improvement in utility after optimization.
Cswitch – Cost of switching from the previous plan to a new plan.
ϵ – Minimum utility improvement threshold for accepting a new plan.
